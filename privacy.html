Privacy Policy – Socho Analytics

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Socho Analytics is used only by the channel owner to read statistics of their own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts.

What it accesses

  • Statistics and analytics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, watch time, subscribers gained), through the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and Instagram Graph API.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is saved only as files on the owner's own computer to measure video performance.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Socho Analytics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
